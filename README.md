# Pulmonary Stress Index (SI) & Ventilator Titration Skill

A production-grade, zero-mock personal agent skill engineered for critical care mechanical ventilation profiling, dynamic airway elastance trajectory modeling, and personalized PEEP/tidal volume titration.

## Features
- **Deterministic Curve Fitting & Classification**: Accurately computes Stress Index exponent $b$ to identify nonlinear elastance changes.
- **VILI Prevention Sentinel**: Direct guidance to prevent cyclic atelectotrauma and alveolar overdistension.
- **Pure Standard Library**: Zero external dependencies; fully portable for edge and clinical AI deployment.

## Clinical References
- Ranieri, V. M., et al. (2000). *Use of the respiratory system stress index to prevent lung injury in patients with acute respiratory distress syndrome*. Anesthesiology, 92(6), 1545-1554.
- Grasso, S., et al. (2007). *Stress index for setting positive end-expiratory pressure in patients with acute respiratory distress syndrome*. Critical Care Medicine, 35(4), 1011-1017.
